# 上臂扭曲
- 创建上臂扭曲骨骼，朝向跟上臂骨骼一致
- ![](attachments/6.0-Twist%20&%20Roll.png)![](attachments/6.0-Twist%20&%20Roll-1.png)
- 为upperarm_roll和upperarm_roll_end骨骼添加单链IK（SingleChainIK）
- ![](attachments/6.0-Twist%20&%20Roll-2.png)
- 将upperarm_roll骨骼和upperarm_roll_ik句柄作为肩膀骨骼的子对象，这样扭曲骨骼跟随肩膀移动
- ![](attachments/6.0-Twist%20&%20Roll-3.png)
- 使用前臂骨骼给upperarm_roll_ik句柄一个点约束，这样Roll骨骼变跟随上臂的YZ轴旋转，而X轴旋转是静止的
- ![](attachments/6.0-Twist%20&%20Roll-4.png)
- 使用上臂骨骼给upperarm_roll_end一个旋转约束，这样upperarm_roll_end跟随上臂骨骼一起旋转
- ![](attachments/6.0-Twist%20&%20Roll-5.png)
- 剩下的就是upperarm_roll_01和upperarm_roll_02的扭曲旋转
	- 使用upperarm_roll和upperarm_roll_end给upperarm_roll_01一个点约束一个朝向约束，并且调整upperarm_roll约束权重为2，upperarm_roll_end的约束权重为1.
	- ![](attachments/6.0-Twist%20&%20Roll-6.png)![](attachments/6.0-Twist%20&%20Roll-7.png)![](attachments/6.0-Twist%20&%20Roll-8.png)
	- 使用upperarm_roll和upperarm_roll_end给upperarm_roll_02一个点约束一个朝向约束，并且调整upperarm_roll约束权重为1，upperarm_roll_end的约束权重为2.
	- ![](attachments/6.0-Twist%20&%20Roll-9.png)![](attachments/6.0-Twist%20&%20Roll-10.png)![](attachments/6.0-Twist%20&%20Roll-11.png)
