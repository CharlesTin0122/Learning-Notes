# Roll
## AnimationChannel
- 选中脚部IK控制器，创建AnimationChannel，命名为FootRoll
- ![](attachments/1.04-The%20Reverse%20Foot-6.png)
- 我们发现创建完成后绑定层级中也出现了AnimChannel
- ![](attachments/1.04-The%20Reverse%20Foot-28.png)
- 这个属性的值为0:1，我们在层级中选中AnimChannel，设置这个值-180:180
- ![](attachments/1.04-The%20Reverse%20Foot-8.png)
- 创建新的AnimChannel名称为ToeRollThreshold，用于设置脚趾在Roll过程中的旋转阈值，取值范围0:180，默认值40.
- ![](attachments/1.04-The%20Reverse%20Foot-9.png)
- 还要记得修改ScriptName，与ChannelName相同即可。要不然在制作动画时看不到这个属性
- ![](attachments/1.04-The%20Reverse%20Foot-10.png)
